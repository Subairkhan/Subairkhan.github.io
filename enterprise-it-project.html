01. Project Overview

This project brings multiple enterprise functions into a consolidated Business Intelligence experience. The dashboards are organized to help users review business performance and explore operational areas such as workforce, client relationships, project delivery, sales, finance, IT service performance, cloud services, training and AI initiatives.

02. Project Objectives

  • Present enterprise performance through an executive-level dashboard.
  • Review workforce information and employee-related measures.
  • Explore client performance, satisfaction and potential risk indicators.
  • Monitor project delivery and sales performance.
  • Review finance, billing and IT operations information.
  • Bring cloud, training and AI-related initiatives into the analytics view.
  • Support business discussions with visual summaries and interactive reporting.

03. Project Workflow

The project is organized around preparing business data, modeling it, and presenting it through focused dashboard pages.

01. Business Area Understanding

  • Excel: Spreadsheet-based data preparation and review.

  • Data Modeling: A dedicated report page shows the model and relationships.

05. Business Areas Covered

  • Executive: Consolidated overview of business performance.
  • Workforce: Employee and workforce-related analysis.
  • Clients: Client analytics and relationship indicators.
  • Projects: Project-related performance and delivery views.
  • Sales & Revenue: Sales performance and revenue reporting.
  • Finance & Billing: Financial and billing-related reporting.
  • IT Operations & SLA: IT service operations and service-level views.
  • Cloud, Training & AI: Digital transformation and capability initiatives.

06. Analytical Value

The dashboard structure supports cross-functional review by placing different enterprise topics in one report. The specific values and conclusions should be interpreted from the visuals in the Power BI file rather than assumed from the page titles alone.

  • The executive view helps reviewers start with an overall business summary.
  • Separate workforce, client and project pages allow more focused functional reviews.
  • Sales and finance pages organize commercial and financial information for analysis.
  • IT operations and SLA reporting gives a dedicated view of service operations.
  • The data model page helps explain the report's data structure and relationships.

07. Business Recommendations

  • Use the executive dashboard as a starting point for regular business performance reviews.
  • Review workforce, client and project pages together when investigating operational changes.
  • Compare sales and finance information before making revenue or billing decisions.
  • Use IT operations and SLA indicators to identify areas that may need service improvement.
  • Validate the underlying data and metric definitions before acting on any dashboard finding.
  • Use the data model page to help maintain consistent definitions across report pages.

08. Business Impact

A consolidated reporting experience can make enterprise information easier to navigate and discuss across teams. This project demonstrates how Power BI can organize business functions into a single portfolio-ready reporting solution.

Executive Visibility

A single entry point gives reviewers a broad view before exploring individual business areas.

Functional Analysis

Dedicated pages make it easier to focus on workforce, clients, projects, sales, finance and IT operations.

Structured Reporting

A separate model view supports understanding of the report structure and relationships.
