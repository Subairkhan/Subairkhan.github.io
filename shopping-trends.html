01. Project Overview

This project analyses 3,900 customer records to understand shopping patterns and purchasing behaviour. The analysis explores product categories, seasons, customer demographics and purchase preferences to identify useful business insights.

02. Project Objectives

  • Understand sales performance across product categories.
  • Compare sales across different seasons.
  • Explore customer demographics and product preferences.
  • Examine discounts, subscriptions and purchase behaviour.
  • Present findings to support business decisions.

03. Project Workflow

A step-by-step process followed to analyze shopping trends and identify useful business insights.

02. Data Analysis with Python

03. SQL Analysis

Used SQL queries, aggregations, subqueries, CTEs and window functions to analyze shopping data.

  • SQL: Queries, aggregations and analytical analysis.
  • Excel: Spreadsheet-based data analysis.
  • Matplotlib and Seaborn: Data visualisation.
  • Power BI: Dashboard and data presentation.

05. Key Findings

  • Clothing recorded the highest total purchase amount at $104,264.
  • Outerwear recorded the lowest total purchase amount at $18,524.
  • Fall had the highest seasonal purchase total at $60,018.
  • Summer had the lowest seasonal purchase total at $55,777.
  • Male customers represented approximately 68% of records.

06. Business Recommendations

  • Review inventory planning for high-demand categories.
  • Investigate the reasons for lower Outerwear sales.
  • Compare seasonal demand before planning promotions.
  • Use customer purchase patterns to guide marketing decisions.
  • Evaluate discounts using purchase data before changing offers.

07. Business Impact

This analysis helps businesses understand customer preferences, product demand and seasonal shopping patterns.

Product Planning

Focus on high-demand categories such as Clothing and identify opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing categories.

Seasonal Strategy

Use seasonal sales trends to support inventory planning and promotional decisions.

Customer Engagement

Use customer purchasing patterns to plan relevant marketing activities and improve the shopping experience.
